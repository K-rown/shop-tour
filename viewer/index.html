Floor plan

Walking around

Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom. The rings on the floor are the places you can walk to: tap one to glide there, keeping the way you are looking. The big arrow walks to the ring in front of you, ‹ and › step along the walk in the order it was recorded. The small map at the top shows where you stand, which way you look and the path that was walked; tap it to open it big, with every stop numbered, and tap a stop there (or a picture in the strip) to jump. Play walks the tour by itself, stop by stop, facing the way it goes — any touch stops it. On a phone, Motion turns the view as you turn the phone; a drag still turns it too.

W/↑ walk toward where you're looking · S/↓ step back · AD/←→ turn · N/P next/previous stop

How to record a walk

If you don't follow this

Every stop is checked for a person after you are painted out. A stop that fails is held back: it is left out of the tour, and the build tells you where the review page is, with the picture and the reason (a person-sized shape still near the camera, something taller than 15 cm where you stood, another person, a face). A leaning stick or an arm sticking out is the usual cause.
